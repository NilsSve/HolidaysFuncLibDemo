  1. Using the demo program

Year Overview

View > Year Overview shows one country's whole year on one page, month by month. It is a FlexTron page - HTML in the program's window - built with the RDCFlexTron library.

The bar at the top

  • The country buttons - the countries the library has holiday names for. Click one to see its year.
  • The year, with buttons for the year before and the year after, and This year to come back.
  • Refresh draws the page again - after a change in the Working Days view.

The year

  • The totals: the working days in the year, the days off for official holidays on weekdays, and the special days that are worked.
  • The twelve months. Each day is marked: a day off for an official holiday, a day off every week (the weekend), a special day that is worked, a day the company is closed, and today. Point at a day to see its name. Each month shows its number of working days.
  • Holidays and special days - the list of the year's named days, and what each one is.

The working days follow the settings of the Working Days view: the days off every week, the closure days and the UK region. Change them there and click Refresh.

The page is built from the library's functions - HolidayName, IsWorkingDay and IsClosedDay - for every day of the year; see Working days.
