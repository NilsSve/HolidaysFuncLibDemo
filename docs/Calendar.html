  1. Using the demo program

Calendar

View > Calendar is an example of a calendar of your own: a table of dates, where each date that is a holiday or special day has its name.

Generate Calendar Dates

In the lower part of the view, enter a Start Date and an End Date, choose the country in For Country, and click Generate Date Records. The demo creates one record for each date in the Holidays table, with the holiday name the library gives for that date and country, and whether it is an official holiday.

Delete All Date Records removes all of them again.

The calendar

The upper part shows the date records of the country in ISO Short:

  • Date, Holiday Name, Holiday (ticked for an official holiday), Day of Week in your language, and Week No.
  • Only Show Holiday Dates hides the ordinary days.
  • Jump to Today's Date moves the grid to today.

Test Holiday Lookup List

This button opens a lookup list of the holidays of the country in a year, where you can select one or more holidays. It is an example of a general-purpose holiday lookup; in your own program, call:

Get PopupHolidayLookup sISO_Short to SelectedHolidays

where SelectedHolidays is a tHoliday[] array. The lookup list is in HolidaysLookup.dg in the library.
