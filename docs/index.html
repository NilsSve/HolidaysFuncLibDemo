    Welcome

    The Holidays and Other Special Dates Library (HolidaysFuncLib) is a free DataFlex library that tells you when holidays and other special days fall, what a date is called, and whether a date is a working day.

    This help describes the demo program, HolidaysFuncLibTest, that shows the library at work, and how to use the library in a program of your own.

    With the library you can:

    • get the date of a holiday in a given year, such as Easter Day 2027 or Thanksgiving in the US;
    • get the name of the holiday or special day a date falls on, in a given country, and whether it is an official holiday;
    • find out whether a date is a working day, add working days to a date, and count the working days between two dates - for delivery dates, due dates and lead times;
    • use a set of date helpers: ISO week numbers, the days in a month, month and day names, and more.

    The demo also shows a country's whole year at a glance - the Year Overview view - and runs in a light or a dark theme (Light and dark theme).

    Countries with holiday names today: the United States (US), the United Kingdom (GB), the Netherlands (NL), Norway (NO), Spain (ES) and Sweden (SE). Adding another country is not hard - see Adding a country.

    To try it, start with Getting started.
