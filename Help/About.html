Holidays and Other Special Dates Library

This program shows the HolidaysFuncLib library at work: the dates of holidays in any year, the names of the holidays in a country, working days, and the date helpers.

  • Date Functions Test - every holiday function, for any year and country;
  • Working Days - IsWorkingDay, AddWorkingDays and WorkingDaysBetween, with their settings;
  • Year Overview - a country's whole year, month by month: a FlexTron page built with the RDCFlexTron library;
  • Calendar and Nations Data - a calendar table of your own, and facts about the nations of the world.

The library and this demo are free, under the MIT license. Help: nilssve.github.io/HolidaysFuncLibDemo. Source code: github.com/NilsSve/Library-HolidaysFuncLib.
